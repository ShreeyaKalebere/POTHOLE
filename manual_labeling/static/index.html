    ⌨️ Keyboard Shortcuts & Guide
    • Enter: Save current boxes and go to next image.
    • N: No damage (clean road) — clears boxes and saves.
    • I: Run trained YOLO model to auto-detect damage.
    • 1 / 2: Switch class (Pothole / Alligator Crack).
    • Click box + Del: Delete selected box.
    • Click & Drag: Draw a new box anywhere.
    • Corner handles: Drag corners to tighten boxes.
    • A / D or arrows: Previous / Next image.
    • X: Delete image (Undo available immediately).
    • Mouse Wheel: Zoom in/out at cursor.
    • Right Drag / Middle Drag: Pan image.
    • F: Fit to screen · H: Toggle hide boxes.
