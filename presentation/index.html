Data Engineering

RoadDamage20K: 10,389 Verified Images

Strict Canonical 2-Class Taxonomy

Eliminated irrelevant classes (manholes, markings, longitudinal lines) to focus exclusively on critical road damage:

Class 0 (Pothole): 6,233 Bounding Boxes

Class 1 (Alligator Crack): 8,358 Bounding Boxes

Indian Road Focus: 4,092 Images (39.4% anchor)

Difficult Negatives: 1,598 Frames (Normal roads)

Zero Video Frame Leakage Defense

Random splitting causes consecutive frames to bleed between train and test, inflating accuracy falsely.

Sequence Group Clustering:
• Train: 7,270 images (70%)
• Val: 1,558 images (15%)
• Test: 1,561 images (15% held-out)

Removed 89 duplicates via SHA-256 and pHash.

Deep Learning Core

Ultralytics YOLO11 Model Innovation

C3k2 Backbone

Cross Stage Partial blocks with customizable kernel sizes, significantly reducing parameter count and FLOPs while preserving deep gradient flow.

SPPF Multi-Scale Neck

Spatial Pyramid Pooling Fast captures small, distant potholes (scale 5x5) alongside broad interconnected pavement fatigue networks (scale 13x13).

Anchor-Free Decoupled Head

Separates classification and coordinate regression. Employs Complete IoU (CIoU) Loss and Distribution Focal Loss (DFL) for sub-pixel boundary accuracy.

Downstream Tracking

ByteTrack: Eliminating Duplicate Reports

The "Infinite Counting" Defect

When a vehicle drives over a single pothole at 30 km/h, the detector sees it across 30 consecutive video frames.

Without tracking: 1 pothole = 30 separate municipal reports!

ByteTrack Two-Stage Association

1. Associates high-confidence boxes ($conf \ge 0.5$) using Kalman Filter predictions.

2. Retains low-confidence boxes ($0.1 \le conf < 0.5$) to recover motion-blurred potholes without spawning new duplicate IDs.

Result: Every defect receives exactly ONE persistent tracking ID.

Heuristic Formulation

Area-Based Severity Decision Boundaries

Pothole Severity (Class 0)

Derived from normalized bounding-box area $A_{norm} = w_{norm} \times h_{norm}$:

Low Severity Area < 2% • Minor surface spalling (< 15 cm)

Medium Severity Area 2% - 6% • Crater 15 - 40 cm (48h patch)

High Severity Area ≥ 6% • Critical crater > 40 cm (Emergency)

Alligator Crack Severity (Class 1)

Calibrated for extensive fatigue mesh:

Low Severity Area < 4% • Hairline crack network (Slurry seal)

Medium Severity Area 4% - 10% • Interconnected lane mesh

High Severity Area ≥ 10% • Base failure (Mill-and-pave)

Regional Case Study

Kolhapur Municipal Corporation: 5-Zone Administrative Partitioning

1. Central & North Kolhapur

• Central (Shahupuri, Laxmipuri): High commercial traffic, market corridors, intense vehicle braking.
• North (Ujalaiwadi, Kasaba Bawada): Panchganga river basin, monsoon waterlogging & silt wash.

2. South & East Kolhapur

• South (Kalamba, Morewadi): Rapid residential development, pipeline trenching & sub-base settling.
• East (Uchgaon, Gokul Shirgaon): MIDC industrial highway corridor, heavy multi-axle freight wear.

3. West & Citywide Admin

• West (Rankala, Phulewadi): Tourist bus transit, lake embankment moisture seepage.
• Citywide (Chief Engineer): Unified municipal command, cross-ward contractor dispatch & budget allocation.

Ward Officer Authentication Workflow: When an engineer authenticates (e.g. Ward Officer - West Kolhapur), the Leaflet GIS map automatically pans to coordinates [16.6920° N, 74.2050° E], scopes municipal KPI cards, and isolates distress records strictly within that administrative jurisdiction.
